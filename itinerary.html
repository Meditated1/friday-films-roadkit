DAY 1 Salina, KS → Oklahoma City, OK ~290 mi · ~4.5 hrs driving I-135 S → I-35 S short, easy start

🏨 Sleep — Oklahoma City (I-40 / Meridian "Restaurant Row" cluster)

All three are reliably cat-friendly suite/extended-stay hotels right off I-40 at Meridian — kitchens are handy with cats. Pet fee ~$50–75/stay. All on Hotels.com (use your points). 🎆 July 4th weekend — book early.

⛽ Fuel stops (Tacoma fills ~every 200 mi)

  • Wichita, KS (I-135, ~mi 90): QuikTrip — last KS fuel, spotless restrooms, big lot
  • Perry, OK (I-35 Exit 185): Love's Travel Stop — top BOTH vehicles off (OK is cheapest, $3.74)
  • Edmond, OK (I-35): Pilot / OnCue — final top-off before OKC

🚻 Rest / diaper breaks

  • Wichita QuikTrip (~1.5 hrs in): first diaper stop, easy on/off I-135
  • OK Welcome Center, I-35 SB mile 225: just past the border — 24/7 restrooms, pet/stretch area

🍽️ Food

  • Cracker Barrel — Edmond, I-35 Exit 137: big lot, high chairs, best family stop near OKC
  • Braum's (KS/OK regional): burgers + fresh ice cream all along I-135/I-35, fast & kid-friendly
  • QuikTrip: genuinely fast grab-and-go with a baby
DAY 2 Oklahoma City, OK → Memphis, TN ~465 mi · ~7 hrs driving I-40 E (via Little Rock) longest day — pad the stops

🏨 Sleep — Memphis EAST (Germantown Pkwy / I-40 Exit 16, Wolfchase) — not downtown

Quiet, big-box-lot suburban cluster — safer & easier with a trailer than downtown. Hampton's lot has a patrolling security guard. All on Hotels.com.

⛽ Fuel stops

  • Henryetta, OK (I-40 Exit 237): Love's — pull-through, restaurant (cheap OK fuel)
  • N. Little Rock, AR (I-40 Exit 161): Pilot/Flying J — huge truck lot, easy trailer
  • West Memphis, AR (I-40 Exit 280): Love's — last AR fuel, fill before TN ($3.93)

🚻 Rest / diaper breaks

  • Sallisaw, OK (I-40 Exit 308): Travel center — deli + restrooms
  • Alma / Fort Smith, AR: AR Welcome Center — clean mid-afternoon stretch
  • West Memphis, AR: Love's — last big stop before the river

🍽️ Food

  • Cracker Barrel — Alma, AR (I-40 Exit 13): great mid-day sit-down reset, big lot, high chairs
  • Cracker Barrel / Chick-fil-A — Little Rock, AR: reliable dinner ~2/3 of the way
  • Whataburger (OK/AR): fast, easy trailer parking
465 miles with a baby — plan 4 stops and one real sit-down meal (Alma or Little Rock Cracker Barrel) to reset everyone.
DAY 3 Memphis, TN → Montgomery, AL ~330 mi · ~5 hrs driving I-22 E → I-65 S easiest day

🏨 Sleep — Montgomery (East Blvd / I-85 EastChase cluster)

EastChase Hampton is the highest-rated hotel of the whole trip (4.7/5). Saturday night — book early. All on Hotels.com.

⛽ Fuel stops

  • Tupelo, MS (I-22 ~Exit 85): Love's/Pilot — fill here, MS is 2nd-cheapest ($3.81)
  • Birmingham, AL (I-65 corridor): Pilot/Flying J before the run south to Montgomery
Note: Buc-ee's at Leeds (I-20) and Athens (I-65 north) are both OFF your southbound route — don't plan around them.

🚻 Rest / diaper breaks

  • Byhalia/Holly Springs, MS: MS Welcome Center / Love's leaving Memphis
  • Tupelo, MS (~1.5 hrs in): Love's + retail — diaper + lunch hub
  • Pelham/Alabaster, AL (I-65): Pilot/Love's before Montgomery

🍽️ Food

  • Cracker Barrel — Tupelo, MS: clean, high chairs, perfect mid-leg lunch
  • Chick-fil-A — Birmingham, AL: best fast-with-a-baby option, off I-65
  • Jim 'N Nick's Bar-B-Q (Birmingham-founded): family-friendly, good parking
DAY 4 Montgomery, AL → Leesburg, FL 🏁 ~470 mi · ~7 hrs driving US-231 → I-10 E → I-75 S (Exit 329) flat all the way home

🏁 Arrival — 34407 Shadewood Circle, Leesburg, FL 34788 (first-month base)

I-75 S to Exit 329 (Wildwood, SR-44), then ~20 min of local roads to 34407 Shadewood Circle, Leesburg 34788. Completely toll-free — no Florida Turnpike needed. Day 4 is long but dead flat (I-10/I-75).

⛽ Fuel stops

  • Dothan, AL (US-231): Love's/Pilot — last AL fuel before FL
  • Lake City, FL (I-10/I-75 jct, Exit 427): Love's — major truck stop, perfect for trailer
  • Ocala, FL (I-75 Exit ~358): top off at any Pilot/RaceTrac before the final hop

🚻 Rest / diaper breaks

  • Troy, AL (US-231, ~1 hr in): early diaper stop on the two-lane stretch
  • Tallahassee / Madison, FL (I-10): FL Welcome Center — clean, 24/7, stretch area
  • Lake City, FL (I-75 Exit 427): last big clean stop before the southbound run

🍽️ Food

  • Cracker Barrel — Dothan, AL (US-231): ideal first real meal of the day
  • Cracker Barrel — Lake City, FL (I-75 Exit 427): late-lunch reset before the final leg
  • Chick-fil-A — Tallahassee, FL (off I-10): fast & spotless
Heads-up: the Ocala Buc-ee's you may have heard about is not open yet (delayed to ~2028) — Lake City Love's + FL Welcome Centers are your reliable substitutes. There is no Buc-ee's on this route.
